AI for accountants, agencies and professional offices

Clients pay for your time and judgment, and a lot of the day goes to the writing around it: recaps after meetings, document requests, status updates, proposals. AI can turn your notes into a clean draft in minutes, so more of your time goes to the advice itself.

Client confidentiality comes first. Check your firm's rules and your professional obligations before you paste client information into any outside service, and leave out anything you wouldn't put in an ordinary email.

Who this is for: Accountants, bookkeepers, tax preparers, agencies, consultants, insurance agents, mortgage brokers and law offices.
