Turn photos of your own salon work into a week of Instagram captions with AI, each ending with one clear way to book. Free prompt and example.

Personal careSaves: most of the writing time on each post (rough estimate)

Who it's for

Hair stylists, barbers, nail and lash techs, estheticians, spas and studios that post their work on Instagram.

The situation today

  • Your camera roll is full of good photos of your work, but writing something under each one is where posting stalls.
  • Generic caption lists online don't mention your services, your town or how to book with you.
  • Rough estimate: with notes ready, drafting and editing five captions takes about 20 minutes.

Time figures marked "rough estimate" are our estimates, not survey data.

The AI-assisted workflow

Step by step

  1. Pick 5 photos or videos of your own work from the last few weeks. Use a client's photo only if they agreed to it being shared.
  2. For each one, jot down the service, what you did, one detail a client would notice and who it suits.
  3. Write down how clients book (link in bio, booking app, DM or phone) and any real openings this week.
  4. Paste your notes into the prompt below and ask for one caption per post.
  5. Edit each caption into your own voice and check every service name, price and opening against your booking app.
  6. Post one a day, then note which captions bring booking messages and reuse what worked.

Copy-paste prompt

I run a [salon/barbershop/nail or lash studio] in [town]. Write Instagram captions for these posts of my own work. Use only the details I give; don't invent services, prices, results, openings or client stories.
How clients book: [link in bio / booking app / DM / phone]
Openings this week (or 'none'): [days and times]
Our voice: [3 words]
Posts:
1) [service] - [what I did] - [one detail a client would notice] - [who it suits]
2) ...

For each post write: a short first line that names the result or the service, 2-3 short sentences, one call to book that matches how clients book, and up to 5 hashtags that name the service and [town]. No client names. No promises about results. If a detail is missing, ask me instead of guessing.

Works in any AI chat assistant. Replace everything in [brackets].

Optional tool

No new tool needed. Draft with any free AI chat assistant and post through the Instagram account and booking link you already use.

Watch-outs

  • Post a client's photo only after they agree, and leave their name out unless they say it's fine.
  • Check every service name, price, opening and result against your booking app. AI can make up details or promise results you can't guarantee.
  • If you try an AI agent that connects to your Instagram account, such as Meta's Muse, read each caption before you approve it. Meta says Muse publishes nothing without your approval.

When not to use AI

  • Replying to a complaint or a comment about a bad experience. Answer that yourself, kindly and briefly.
  • Captions about skin, scalp or other health conditions. Write those yourself and make no promises about results.

Quick answers

What should I write in a salon Instagram caption?

Lead with the result or the service, add two or three short sentences about what you did and who it suits, then one clear way to book. Leave out client names.

Can AI write my salon's Instagram captions?

Yes, if you give it your own notes on each photo. Tell it to use only your details, then check every service, price and opening before you post.
