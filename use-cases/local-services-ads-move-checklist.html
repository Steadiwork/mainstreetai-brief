Local Services Ads to Google Ads: what to save first

Short answer: Before your Local Services Ads account moves into Google Ads, screenshot or copy your performance reports, because Google says they won't carry over. Write down your budget, bidding and settings, have an AI assistant turn them into a one-page baseline, then recheck every setting and compare results after the move.

Setup time
about 20 minutes, once (estimate)
Skill level
Some tech comfort
Cost
$0 with a free AI chat assistant and your own screenshots.
Best for
Trades owners who get leads from Google Local Services Ads

Updated Oct 27, 2026 · By the Main Street AI Brief team · Facts checked against the sources below.

Google is moving Local Services Ads into Google Ads, and old performance reports won't carry over. Save them, record your settings, and recheck after. Who it's for: Plumbers, HVAC, electricians, cleaners, lawn care, roofers, pest control, movers and other service businesses that pay for leads through Google Local Services Ads. Saves: a clean before-and-after record (rough estimate)

The situation today

  • Google says existing Local Services Ads campaigns will be moved automatically, over the coming months, into a Performance Max campaign type that keeps pay-per-lead.
  • The first phase began in August 2026 for select U.S. home and storefront service advertisers, including plumbing, HVAC, electrical, house cleaning, lawn care, roofing, pest control and moving. Google says it expands in late 2026 and reaches remaining categories and non-U.S. accounts in 2027.
  • Your past leads, messages and call recordings move over, Google says. Your past performance reports (spend, impressions, weekly spend) do not.
  • Rough estimate: 20 minutes now saves you from guessing later whether your leads went up or down after the move.

Time figures marked "rough estimate" are our estimates, not survey data.

The AI-assisted workflow

Step by step

  1. Open your Local Services Ads dashboard and screenshot or copy your performance reports now. Google says they won't move to Google Ads and that a download page will be available 'soon'. Once you're moved, the old dashboard redirects to Google Ads.
  2. Write down your current settings: weekly budget, bidding (a max cost per lead, or different targets by service), service types, service areas, ad schedule, photos and callouts.
  3. Paste the monthly totals and settings, without any customer details, into the prompt below. Check that the AI's table matches your screenshots line by line.
  4. Watch your inbox. Google says the account administrator gets an email 14 days before the move date, a reminder 7 days later, and another email once it's done.
  5. After the move, recheck every setting in Google Ads. Google says weekly budgets become daily (weekly divided by 7), manual bidding and per-service target cost per lead go away, and BBB callouts are no longer supported.
  6. Compare leads and cost per lead with your baseline. Google says to allow up to two weeks for the move to finish and results to settle.

Copy-paste prompt

Help me make a one-page record of my Google Local Services Ads results before my account moves to Google Ads. Use only the numbers I give you. Do not guess, fill gaps or add advice I didn't ask for.

My trade and service area: [e.g. plumbing, north county]
Monthly totals I copied from my reports (month, leads, amount spent, cost per lead): [paste, one month per line]
My current weekly budget: $[amount]
Bidding I use now: [e.g. max cost per lead $X, or target cost per lead by service]
Settings I want to recheck after the move: [service types, service areas, ad schedule, callouts, phone number]

Give me:
1) A table of my monthly totals, with an average for the period.
2) My weekly budget divided by 7, and that daily number times 30.4, labeled 'Google says monthly spend is capped at this'.
3) A checklist of my settings to recheck in Google Ads after the move.
If any number is missing or doesn't add up, list it under 'Check this' instead of guessing.

Works in any AI chat assistant. Replace everything in [brackets].

Optional tool

No extra tool needed: Google moves the account itself, and a free AI chat assistant plus your screenshots covers the record-keeping.

Watch-outs

  • Keep customer names, phone numbers, message history and call recordings out of the AI chat. Paste only totals and settings.
  • AI can misread numbers from a screenshot. Check every figure in its table against the original before you rely on it.
  • Google says all account users are opted in to lead email notifications by default after the move, whatever you set before. Check who gets them under Admin, Notifications.
  • Business name, address and hours now sync one way from your Google Business Profile. Google says big changes to name or address trigger a review that typically takes 24 to 48 hours, and your campaign may pause during it.

When not to use AI

  • Deciding your new budget or bids. That's your call, based on your own numbers and margins.
  • Questions about your specific account. Google lists a migration support line on its help page: +1-844-205-0011, Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM EST.

Quick answers

Will my old Local Services Ads leads carry over to Google Ads?

Google says yes: past leads, including contact details, message history and older call recordings, transfer to the new Leads page in Google Ads. Past performance reports do not, so save those first.

What happens to my weekly budget?

Google says your average weekly budget is divided by 7 to set a daily average budget, and monthly spend is capped at that daily budget times 30.4.
