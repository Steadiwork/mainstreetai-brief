How to write restaurant menu descriptions

Short answer: List each dish with the ingredients you actually use, the price, and which of the nine major allergens it contains. Paste that list into an AI chat with a prompt that says to use only your facts. Check every word against your recipes before printing or posting the descriptions.

Setup time
about 25 minutes (estimate)
Skill level
Beginner
Cost
$0 with a free AI chat assistant.
Best for
Cafés, restaurants and bakeries refreshing a menu

Updated Nov 6, 2026 · By the Main Street AI Brief team · Facts checked against the sources below.

Write appetizing menu descriptions from your own ingredients and allergen notes with AI, then check every claim before the menu goes out. Who it's for: Restaurant, café and bakery owners who need clearer menu copy without inventing ingredients or allergen claims. Saves: most of a rewrite afternoon (rough estimate)

The situation today

  • Menu lines often stop at the dish name and a price, so guests guess what's in it.
  • Generic AI menu copy adds fluff words and sometimes ingredients you don't use.
  • Rough estimate: rewriting a full menu by hand can take an afternoon; a checked AI draft cuts that to one sitting.

Time figures marked "rough estimate" are our estimates, not survey data.

The AI-assisted workflow

Step by step

  1. Open a blank note. For each dish write: name, exact price, main ingredients you use, spice or portion note, and which major allergens it contains from your recipes.
  2. Mark any dish you call gluten-free, vegan or local only if that claim is true for how you make it today.
  3. Paste the list into the prompt below and ask for a short description per dish.
  4. Have the cook or whoever knows the recipes check every ingredient word and allergen line. Fix anything the AI added.
  5. Drop the checked copy into your printed menu, POS, QR menu or delivery apps. Keep the note file for the next specials update.

Copy-paste prompt

I run a [café/restaurant/bakery] in [town]. Write a short menu description for each dish below. Use only the facts I give. Do not invent ingredients, sourcing, cooking methods, calories or claims. Do not call anything gluten-free, allergen-free, vegan, organic or local unless I say so. For each dish: one appetizing sentence (under 25 words) that names the main ingredients, then a line that starts with "Contains:" listing only the major allergens I listed (or "Contains: none of the nine major allergens" if I listed none). Keep every price exactly as written. If a fact is missing, write [CHECK] instead of guessing.

Dishes (name | price | ingredients | allergens from my list):
[paste your list]

Works in any AI chat assistant. Replace everything in [brackets].

Optional tool

Watch-outs

  • AI must never decide what is in a dish. Allergen lines come from your recipes and supplier labels, checked by whoever cooks.
  • The FDA lists nine major food allergens for packaged-food labeling: milk, eggs, fish, crustacean shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, soybeans and sesame. Restaurant menus are also subject to state and local rules; check those before you print.
  • Never let AI invent "gluten-free," "organic," "local" or similar claims. Use those words only when you can back them up.

When not to use AI

  • Writing the legal allergen board or packaged-food labels required by law. Use your recipes, supplier specs and local rules for that, and check with an attorney or your health department when needed.

Quick answers

Can AI write my restaurant menu descriptions?

Yes, if you give it your real ingredients, prices and allergen notes and check every line before printing or posting. Never let it invent what's in a dish.

What allergens should I list on a menu?

Start with the nine major U.S. food allergens the FDA names for packaged foods: milk, eggs, fish, crustacean shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, soybeans and sesame. Confirm state and local menu rules too.
