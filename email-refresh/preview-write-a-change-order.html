Turn a customer's extra-work request into a signed change order

Setup: about 15 minutes · Saves: most of the writing on each change order (rough estimate)

  1. Write down exactly what the customer asked for, in their words, with the date.
  2. Decide the price and any extra days yourself, then paste them and the request into the prompt below.
  3. Check every line: scope, what's not included, price, new total and schedule.
  4. Get it signed before you start the extra work. Rules on written change orders vary by state, so check with your attorney or licensing board.
  5. Save the signed copy and add the change to your final invoice.

Copy-paste prompt:

Write a one-page change order for my [trade] business. Use only these facts; don't add work, prices, dates or terms.
Original job and price: [job, $amount]
Customer's request, in their words: [paste]
My price for the extra work: $[amount]
Extra days: [number or none]
Payment terms for this change: [terms]

Include: change order number [#] and date, the scope in plain English plus what's not included, a table of original price, this change and new total, the effect on schedule and payments, the line 'Work on this change starts only after we both sign', and signature lines for both of us. If anything is missing, list it under 'Questions for me' instead of guessing.

Privacy: Leave out the customer's name, address, phone and payment details; add them in your own form afterward.

Example output (illustrative, not real data):

Change Order #2, Example Plumbing Co.

Scope: Install the customer-supplied garbage disposal and connect it to the existing wall switch. Not included: new wiring.

ItemAmount
Original contract$[original price]
This change$[your price]
New total$[new total]

Schedule: no extra days. Work starts only after we both sign.
