Ask every customer for a Google review in 15 minutes

Today you'll write a short text and email that ask customers for an honest Google review, plus the two Google rules that keep those reviews from being removed.

Do this today

Write a short text and email that ask every customer for an honest Google review, with your review link

Setup about 15 minutes · Saves the rewriting each time you ask (rough estimate)

  1. On a computer, open your Business Profile, select Read reviews, then Get more reviews, and copy your review link. Save the QR code too if you want one for the counter.
  2. Note when customers are happiest to hear from you (right after pickup, the day after a job, at checkout) and how they usually reach you.
  3. Paste the prompt below into your AI assistant and fill in the brackets.
  4. Edit the drafts so they sound like you, then save them as text shortcuts or email templates.
  5. Send the same ask to every customer. Google's policy bans asking only happy customers and offering anything (discounts, freebies) in return.

Copy-paste prompt 

I run a [type of business] in [town]. Write two short messages asking a customer for an honest Google review.
Message 1: a text under 40 words.
Message 2: an email under 90 words with a subject line.
When I send it: [right after the visit / the day after the job].
My review link: [paste link]
How I talk: [3 words].

Rules:
- Ask for an honest review, not a 5-star one, and don't suggest what to write.
- No discounts, gifts, entries or anything in return.
- Mention one real thing about the visit only if I add it here: [optional detail].
- Don't invent anything else about the customer or the visit.
- Make it easy to say no.

Privacy: Keep customer names, phone numbers and anything private about the job out of the AI chat; add the name yourself when you send.
