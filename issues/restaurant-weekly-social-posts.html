Turn this week's specials, hours and photos into a week of social posts in one sitting

Setup about 30 minutes · Saves most of the daily 'what do we post?' time (rough estimate)

  1. Once a week, list your specials with exact prices and days, plus your hours and any closures.
  2. Pick 5 photos of your own food and space, and write one line on what's in each shot.
  3. Paste your list and photo notes into the prompt below and ask for one post per day.
  4. Check every dish, price, day and allergen word against your menu and POS, then edit into your own voice.
  5. Schedule the posts, then note which ones bring orders or questions.

Copy-paste prompt 

I run a [café/restaurant/bakery] in [town]. Write [5] [Instagram/Facebook] posts for the week of [dates], one per day. Use only the facts below; don't invent dishes, prices, ingredients, hours or quotes, and don't call anything allergen-free or gluten-free unless I say so.
Specials (name - price - days - one detail): [list]
Hours and closures: [hours]
How people order: [walk in / online link / phone]
Photos (one line each): [list]
Our voice: [3 words]

For each day give: the photo to use, a first line naming the dish, 1-2 short sentences, one call to action and up to 3 hashtags including [town]. Keep each price exactly as written. Include one behind-the-scenes post. If a detail is missing, ask me instead of guessing.

Privacy: Leave out customer names, photos of guests who didn't agree, staff schedules or personal details, and any sales or supplier figures you don't want public.
